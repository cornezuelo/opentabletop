# Roll modes

The **Roll modes** tab shows the roll modes of the packs the system brings: its own, and read-only a bundled dependency's (like Core's advantage). **New roll modes** adds them to its own pack. The YAML: [Roll modes](../technical/07-kinds.md#roll-modes).

Each has its name, how many **rolls**, which total it **keeps** (`highest`, `lowest`, `middle`) and the modes it **cancels**.
