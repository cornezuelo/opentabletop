# YAML

The **YAML** tab shows the files of the system's parts, one button per file when they're in several (e.g. `travel.yaml` and `system.yaml`). Anything the forms don't cover can be written there; every kind is in [Kinds of definition](../technical/07-kinds.md), and every piece of syntax in [Syntax](../technical/09-syntax.md).

- The forms change these files, keeping your comments and order.
- Any problem is marked at its line; a button above the forms says how many problems there are and opens it.
- On the Generic rules, which can't be edited, it shows them as a pack would write them (their travel rules and their characters' sheet), to read or copy from.
