# Sub-maps

A place on a map can lead into a map of its own, a **sub-map**: the dungeon under a hill, the town on a crossroads, the caves below a mountain, a tunnel under a ridge. A sub-map belongs to the campaign of the map it hangs from (the same world clock, factions and Oracle) and may have sub-maps of its own. It has one way to the map above or **several**: a tunnel with a mouth at each end is **crossed**, in by one and out by another. The Grey Marches show both ([The Grey Marches](../packs/02-grey-marches.md)): **The Barrow-halls**, under the Barrow-halls' cell (1109) of the Hollow Hills, with one way down, and **The old clan mine**, under their mountains, with three: its mouth (1208), a shaft on the mountainside, the Windhole (1310), and its east adit at Hollow Gate (1312).

## Making one

- Select a cell and, in its panel, under **Ways into other maps**, click **New sub-map here**. An empty dungeon ([squares](01-getting-started.md#hexes-or-squares) of 3 m and the dungeon palette, as **New map → Dungeon**) is kept in **Maps**, named after the cell (or _Under the cell_), in the same campaign (**Map settings → Cells** turns it into hexes). The cell now shows its way in: a dark arch with steps going down.
- Open it with **Open** in the same place. It's a map like any other: draw it with the same tools, at the scale you choose in **Map settings** (**km per cell**, a square's side on a map of squares: `0.01` is ten metres, a room or a passage; `0.5` half a kilometre, a long gallery).
- Its **Map settings** say where it hangs, under **Inside**: the map above (click it to open it), and then each of its **ways**: **From**, the place above that leads in; its **Entrance**, where the way arrives and leaves from, the sub-map's middle cell at first (select another cell and click **Make … the entrance** to move it; it shows an arch with a chevron up); and its **Kind of way**, the kind of connection going in and out by it is, as the system names it (below). Each way has its own kind: a stair at one end, a shaft at the other.
- **More ways in**: on the map above, select another cell and, under **Ways into other maps**, choose the sub-map in **Another way into…** and click **Add**. The cell is one more way in; its entrance starts at the first way's, to be moved in the sub-map's **Map settings**. **Remove this way** there takes one away (a sub-map keeps one at least).
- In **Maps**, sub-maps are listed under the map they hang from, one step in (↳). The bar at the top of a sub-map says where it hangs: _The Hollow Hills › The Barrow-halls_, each name opening its map.

## Going in on a trip

- With the party on a cell that leads into a sub-map, the **Play** panel (and the cell's panel) offers **Go in ↗**: the way's other end becomes the destination, and the march takes it as any other step. On a sub-map, each entrance offers **Go out ↗** the same way, back to its own place above.
- **Through it**: a destination beyond a sub-map with several ways is reached through it if that's the way the route finds best, as it chooses between any two ways over land. If a tunnel's galleries are quicker than the mountains above, a click on its far side heads in by one mouth, walks the galleries cell by cell and comes out by the other.
- Going through a way is a step of its kind: what it costs and does is the system's, as for any [connection](04-roads-and-rivers.md#connections) (its `extra` minutes, its **Open when** / **Closed when**, checks that read `connection.kind`). The step measures no distance: with no kind, it takes nothing more than the way of travelling's time a step, if it has one. If a way's entrance is in a [room](03-regions.md#rooms), the way arrives in the room.
- Once through, the trip **goes on in the other map**, which opens: the same trip, party, journal and time, still heading where it was (the route is planned again there; if no way of that map reaches the destination, it's given up). The party stands on the entrance of the way it took (or, coming out, on that way's cell above). Places of the map it left are named with that map's name in the journal (_The Hollow Hills · 1109_).
- The map it left keeps the party where it went in, and its **Play** panel says where the trip went on, with **Open it**. Clicking a cell there does nothing while the trip is away.

What a sub-map is for and how it plays is the system's: its terrains, checks and actions read its places as they read any map's. For the file format, see [File formats](../technical/02-file-formats.md).
