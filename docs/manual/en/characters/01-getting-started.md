# Getting started

The Characters app keeps your **roster**: the characters this browser keeps, of any system. Each one is made with its system's characters' sheet (its values, conditions and kinds of relation), the sheet the system names in Systems ([Sheet](../systems/07-sheet.md)). A roster character can travel on a map in the Map app or on a trip in Travel played with its system, in one place at a time, and what happens to it there comes back to the roster. When a system has a sheet for the party itself, the roster keeps **parties** too: that sheet and the characters in it ([Parties](#parties)).

## The screen

- **Header**: the app switcher (nine dots), the gear (**Preferences**: language and notes app, shared by every app) and **?** for this manual. The small tabs on the edges of the middle column (‹ ›) fold the lists and the help column away, and bring them back.
- **The lists** (left), two tabs alike:
  - **Characters**: **New character**, **Import…** and the roster.
  - **Bestiary**: the templates of every system's bestiaries ([below](#the-bestiary)), and **New template ↗**, which opens the Systems app where templates are made.

  Each is grouped by system, and within a system by the sheet each entry is made with when it has more than one (in the Grey Marches, _Companion_ and _Foe_): a character or template is listed with the system whose sheet it is, its characters' or another of its sheets (a beast's, for a copy of a template); **Other sheets** gathers those whose system isn't loaded. Above the characters, **Campaign** chooses which show: by default those playing in the campaign you're playing (**Playing**, in the bar at the top: [The campaign you play in](../campaign/01-getting-started.md#the-campaign-you-play-in)), in its trips or on its maps' tokens; **All campaigns**, the whole roster; **No campaign**, those playing nowhere. A search box finds them by name, id or tag, and under it the tags they have, as pills: choose some to see only those with every tag chosen (`npc`, or `npc` and `bandit`); **×** shows them all again. A system tells its player characters from its NPCs by their sheets and by the tags you give them. At the bottom, **Make or edit systems** opens the Systems app.

- **The character** or **the template** (center): the one chosen in the list.

Labels underlined with dots have an explanation: click one and the help column opens on it.

## A new character

**New character** asks for the **system** (only systems with a characters' sheet are listed; the one you last chose in any app comes first) and a **name**, and makes the character with the sheet's starting values. When the system has a party sheet, **Sheet** chooses between a character and **a party** ([Parties](#parties)). Its **id** comes from the name (`Old Tobin` → `old-tobin`; `old-tobin-2` if the roster already has one) and doesn't change: it's how rules and tables reach it once it travels (`characters.old-tobin.values.health`) and how relations point at it (`character:old-tobin`).

## The character

- **Duplicate** makes a copy with its values, in the roster only; **Export** downloads it as an `.otd.json` file; **Delete** removes it from the roster (after asking).
- **Sheet**: the sheet it's made with and its system, with **Edit the sheet in Systems →**. A character whose sheet isn't loaded (its pack removed, or a personal-use pack in another build) shows its values as they are, and can't be changed until the sheet is back.
- **Made from**, for a copy of a template: the template and its bestiary, with **Open in the Bestiary**. Its numbers were rolled when it was made; from then on it's a character like any other, edited here, and changing the template doesn't change it.
- **Id**: how it's read, as above.
- **Where it plays**: the map or trip it travels on now, with **Open**, _in the campaign …, without a trip_, or _only in the roster_; under it, the campaign that map or trip is in, with **Open in Campaign ↗** (the [Campaign](../campaign/01-getting-started.md) app).
- **In a campaign** (when you have campaigns): **a quick game**, in a campaign without a trip or token. The campaign's **Characters** tab lists it, **Campaign** above the list finds it there, and a new trip of the campaign in Travel offers its party (a party is put there with its members). It's still in one place at a time: putting it in a campaign takes it out of the trip or token it plays in (asked first; that place keeps its copy, no longer linked), and taking it on a trip takes it out of the campaign's cast without a trip. To have the same people in two campaigns, **Duplicate** them (a character, or a party and its members one by one). **None** leaves it only in the roster.
- **Notes**: its page in your notes app (a path such as `People/Old Tobin`); **↗** opens it. The notes app is chosen in **Preferences**. Its story, looks and secrets live there, not here.
- Under them, its card, the same as in a trip: its values, each group a card, each value drawn as its sheet says (a number, a bar, boxes, dots, a dial, a named level: click a box, dot or segment to fill up to it; see [How values are shown](../systems/07-sheet.md#how-values-are-shown)), its conditions, its tags (each a pill; typing suggests those its system's characters and templates already use, so `npc` is spelled the same everywhere and the lists' filter finds them) and its relations (to places, regions, cells or other roster characters). What holds by itself is shown but not edited: a value **made of others** (Wounds, from Health) and a condition that **holds while** something is true (Terrified, while Dread is full) follow what they're made of; change that instead. A condition that reads the world (Seasick, while the party travels by boat) follows it on a trip; here, away from any trip, it stays as it was last seen ([Sheet](../systems/07-sheet.md#how-sheets-work)).
- Its **Items**, when its system gives characters items: what it carries, grouped by the places its sheet declares (hands, body, pack…, each with how much of its room is taken, in red when over) and the containers it carries (_In Backpack_). Each item has **How many**, the numbers its system gives items (a torch's uses as dots), where to move it (drag its handle ⠿ onto a place, or pick one: only the places it fits, with room left where the sheet takes no more than a place's room; hold Shift as you drop to move only one of a stack) and × to drop it (with what's inside it). When the sheet gives a **Room in all**, **Carried** above shows how much is carried against it. **Add an item** suggests the system's catalogues by name; any other name makes one by hand; what has no room left is refused (_No room left for…_). An item marked _No room for it_ came on a trip with nowhere to go: drop something, or move it to a place. **Places of its own** adds a place only this character has (a tail, a third arm). Values made of the items (a load) follow them at once ([Items](../systems/08-items.md)).

## The bestiary

The **Bestiary** tab lists the creatures and kinds of people your systems can make, as **templates**: each a character of one of the system's sheets whose rolls (`{{2d4}}`) are made again for every copy ([Bestiary](../systems/18-bestiary.md)). Pick one in the list (a variant has the tags of the template it's like, so in the Grey Marches filtering by `pack` finds the pack leader too):

- Under its name, its system, the sheet it's made with, and **Edit in Systems ↗**. **In the roster**: **Make a character** makes one character of it and opens it, named by the template's **Names from** if it has one (a recurring NPC: a bandit who comes back as _Hrolf_), else after the template; **Make copies** makes that many at once (the wolves of tonight's fight), named the same way or numbered (_Grey wolf 1_, _2_…). Each has its own rolls and remembers the template it came from (**Made from**); they're listed with their system and edited like any character. Under the buttons, how many the roster has made from it, with **Delete them all** to clear them away when the encounter is over (asked first; one that plays somewhere leaves its trip or token its own copy, no longer linked).
- Its card shows what the template says, as it is: **Named as** `present.<id>` (how tables and effects make it in a trip: `present.wolf: 3`), **Values** (a number or a roll; empty takes the sheet's default, shown faint), **Conditions**, **Tags**, **Items** from the system's catalogues, the one it is a **Variant of**, the generator its **Names** come from, what becomes of copies **Left behind** and its **Token** (an icon, a colour, and the kind of token it's drawn as on a map: PC, NPC or Enemy, the map's own kinds).
- Templates are part of their system, like its sheets, so they are made and changed in the Systems app only ([Bestiary](../systems/18-bestiary.md)): **Edit in Systems ↗** opens its system's **Bestiary** tab at this template, unfolded; **New template ↗** opens that tab for the system of the template shown (else the first with a bestiary). A bundled system is read-only there: **Edit a copy** first. What you change only affects copies made afterwards: those already made are characters of the roster, edited here like any other.

## Parties

When a system has a sheet for the party itself (the Grey Marches' **Company**: morale, fatigue, hirelings; Core's **Party**, with its morale), a **party** is a roster entry of that sheet with **members**: characters of the roster, in order. It's listed with its system, under its sheet, like any character, and made with **New character** (**Sheet**: _a party_).

- **Members**, on its page, lists them with links. While it plays nowhere, **Add a member** adds a character of the system's characters' sheet, and **×** takes one out (they stay in the roster). **A character is in one party at a time**: adding one that's in another party asks first, and takes it out of that one. A character's page says which party it's in (**Party**, with a link).
- On a trip, the party is the trip's **The party** ([The party](../travel/02-playing.md#the-party)): taken there with **From the roster** on its card, its members come with it. While it plays, that trip keeps the list: its characters linked to the roster are the members, and its page says so instead of offering changes. It's carried as it is, from one trip to the next: 3 rations left at the end of one are 3 rations at the start of the next.
- **Duplicate** copies the party, with its values and items, without members (a character is in one party at a time).
- **Split the party** (while it plays nowhere and has members): some go their own way. Tick **Who goes** and name the new party. It starts **as any new party of its sheet** (its starting values, nothing else), and this one keeps everything, so nothing is made out of nothing: for each value, say what **Stays** and what **Goes** (**=** gives the new one the same, for something both keep, like a reputation; **½** half each, like silver); for each item, how many go (**½** half). An item that goes whole takes what's inside it. The new party is made in the roster, playing nowhere, and opens.

## On trips

A roster character travels in **one place at a time**: a map's trip in Map, a trip in Travel, or a token on a map (an NPC who comes back). It joins with **From the roster** in the trip's **Party** tab: with the party, or under **Here** as one of those present (a creature made from the bestiary, an NPC met on the way); or with a token's **Sheet**, and a character of a trip joins the roster with **Keep in the roster** on its card ([Characters on a trip](../travel/02-playing.md#characters)). From then on the place and the roster are kept in step: what happens on the trip is written here after every step, and what you change here reaches the trip (now, if it's open in another tab; otherwise when it next opens). **Where it plays** says where it is, with **Open**. Taking it to another place asks first, and the place it leaves keeps its own copy, no longer linked. Taken out of the party, or with its trip or token deleted, it stays here, travelling nowhere. A [party](#parties) travels the same way: on its card, **The party** in the trip's **Party** tab.

## Files

**Export** writes an OTD file (`.otd.json`) with its `characters`: its values as `stats`, its tags, its note, and its sheet, conditions, relations and items in `ext.character` (see [File formats](../technical/02-file-formats.md)). **Import…** adds the characters of such a file: one exported here, or a map saved by Map (its party's characters). Characters whose id the roster already has get a new one. Characters that aren't made with a sheet (a map's plain tokens) are left out.

The roster lives in this browser (`opentabletop.characters`), like your packs and maps: a [backup](../technical/05-backups.md) includes it. Export the characters you care about to keep them safe, or several at once, with the trips they play in, with the app switcher's [**Export…**](../technical/05-backups.md#exporting-and-importing-some-of-it) (a party there asks for its members too).
