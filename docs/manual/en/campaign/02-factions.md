# Factions

The powers of the campaign's world (a kingdom, a clan, a guild), when its system has them (`kind: factions`, made in Systems → [Factions](../systems/08-factions.md)). Each is a sheet of values and conditions, like a character's, kept by the campaign, and **land**: the hexes it holds on each of the campaign's maps. They are the same factions the Hexmapper's World panel shows for a map of the campaign ([Factions](../hexmapper/12-world.md#factions)).

- **Bring in its factions** (when the campaign has none yet): their sheets come to the campaign, and each the land its system says it starts with on the map chosen. With no map in the campaign yet, only their sheets come.
- **Their land on**: the map whose land you see, in a small map of it (each hex in its terrain's colour, each faction's land in its own, with the colours named under it). With several maps in the campaign, choose one; by default it's the map of the trip going on, else the first. **Paint in the Hexmapper ↗** opens that map, to paint land by hand (World → Factions → **Paint its land**).
- **Give them their starting land here**: on a map with no land of theirs (one that joined the campaign after they came), each faction gets the land its system says it starts with there: every hex of its starting regions, by name, and its starting hexes only if it's the example map they're for. Their sheets don't change.
- Each faction, with its colour, its name and how many hexes it holds on that map: open it for what the system says it is and its sheet (change its values and conditions by hand when the story says so). The address `#/campaign/<id>/factions/<faction>` opens one.
- **World turn**: every faction takes its turn now, in order, as in the Hexmapper: what comes up changes them, their land **on the map chosen**, and the progress clocks; each result goes to the timeline. Its explanation is in **What it does**, beside it.
- **Turns by themselves, every N days** (when the system says how often): turns come as the clock moves on, here in **World**, on a map or with a trip. Untick it to take them only with **World turn**.
- **Take the factions out of the campaign** forgets their sheets, and their land on every map of it (after asking).

Land only changes on one map at a time: the map chosen here, or the map being played in the Hexmapper. If that map is open in the Hexmapper, it takes the new land at once (unless it has changes of its own not saved yet: the last one saved wins). With no map in the campaign, world turns change the sheets and clocks but no land. The Grey Marches' factions (the Iron Clans, the Vale of Ashford, the garrison of Fort Keld) show all of this on their example map ([The Grey Marches](../packs/02-grey-marches.md)).
