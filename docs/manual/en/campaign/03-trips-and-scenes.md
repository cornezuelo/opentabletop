# Trips and scenes

Both are ways of **playing**: you take actions, the system's checks and tables answer, a fight goes in turns, the journal tells what happened and the campaign's world clock moves on. They're the same panel with the same tabs. What tells them apart is whether the party **goes somewhere**.

## A trip

A **trip** is the party on the way: it has a place where it stands, a route, a destination and the days it takes. Marching spends hours, tires the party, eats its food and rolls what the way brings (an encounter on the road, the weather, getting lost). Use one when **where the party goes** matters.

- **On a map** (Map's Play): the party is a token on the map's cells, the route is drawn, and what's discovered appears on it. Its trip is the map's.
- **Without a map** (the Travel app): a way told as a list of places and distances, for a journey you don't want to draw.

## A scene

A **scene** is the party **staying where it is**: no route, no march, no destination. The campaign's cast stands at one place with whoever they meet, and you play what happens there: a fight, a parley, a search, a night in an inn. Use one when **what happens here** matters, and the way doesn't. Scenes are played in the Campaign app (**Scenes**), and the Oracle's **Open a scene ↗** starts one with the people a result brings.

## Which one?

| You want to…                                                 | Play                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Cross the land, day after day, with what the way brings      | a trip (on a map, or in Travel) |
| Explore a dungeon or a ruin cell by cell                     | a trip on its map               |
| Fight the wolves the Oracle just rolled, with no map at hand | a scene                         |
| Play a meeting, a parley or a night in town                  | a scene                         |

## What they share

- **The panel**: the same actions, **Party** tab, journal, fights, rolls of yours, Options.
- **The world**: the campaign's world clock, its factions and its Oracle; the time either one takes moves the clock.
- **The characters**: the roster's characters play in one or the other, and what happens to them goes back to the roster as it happens.
- **One at a time**: in a campaign, one trip or scene is going on at a time (**Playing** says which).

## What differs

|              | A trip                                                     | A scene                                                                         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Where        | A place, and a way to a destination                        | One place, going nowhere                                                        |
| Moving on    | March, forced march, by boat…                              | No moving actions                                                               |
| Played in    | Map (on its map) or Travel                                 | Campaign                                                                        |
| When it ends | When you stop it (a trip can be paused and taken up again) | **End the scene**: the cast goes back to the campaign, its journal is discarded |
