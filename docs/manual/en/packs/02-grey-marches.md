# The Grey Marches

> The old kingdom ends where the road gives out. Past Ashford, the Keld Road crosses the river on a toll bridge and climbs to Fort Keld, whose garrison hasn't been paid in a year. North, the Greywood keeps its own counsel — lights between the trees at night, and something large asleep under the roots. South, the Hollow Hills ring with bandits, and the Saltmere lies flat and grey, crossed only by Brenna's ferry. East of the hills, nobody has drawn the map.

**The Grey Marches** is a small frontier to play and to learn from. It's a bundled pack (MIT) with its own **travel system** and an **example map**, and between them they use every feature of OpenTabletop. Read its files in the Oracle app as worked examples: each one has comments saying what it shows, and every table, oracle, generator and deck says in its description what it is for and which features it shows ("Shows: d66 dice…"), so the Oracle's list doubles as a tour.

## Playing it

1. In the Hexmapper, **Maps → Example maps → The Grey Marches**. It opens as one of your maps; open it again later to go on, or start it fresh.
2. **Play** (<kbd>P</kbd>): the map opens ready, **With rules**, playing **The Grey Marches** (its system in **Map settings → Map → System**), with discovery on. Choose a season if you like (**New trip**) and click a destination: the party starts in Ashford. It's the Company: Kael, Mara and Old Tobin, in the trip's **Characters** section, where you choose who acts (whoever leads a ford, whoever tends the wounded) and see their health, rations and dread; the party's Survival, Charisma, Navigation and Stealth are theirs.
3. Try the road to Fort Keld (the toll), the path to the shrine (the ford), the trail to the Grey Stones (a landmark that waits for you), a night in the Greywood, or the ferry across the Saltmere: at Brenna's shore, switch the travel mode to **By boat**; it only goes on water and coast, so switch back to **On foot** to land.
4. Head east, into the blank hexes: they are discovered as you go (untick **Discover the map as you travel** to keep them blank).

The same system plays without a map in the Travel app.

## What is rolled, and when

Every check is in `travel.yaml`; the journal says each one as it comes up.

| Check               | When                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Table                                |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Weather             | Every dawn                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `sky` (weather model, by season)     |
| Getting lost        | Every dawn, unless you set off along a road or river, or by boat                                                                                                                                                      | `getting-lost` (+ Navigation)        |
| Encounter           | Entering by day a hex whose danger is over the party's Stealth (the Greywood, the Hollow Hills), unless you came by road; or resting where danger is 3 or more (`at: [hex-enter, rest]`, told apart by `trip.moment`) | `encounter` (by day)                 |
| Toll                | Entering Keld Bridge (0909) by road                                                                                                                                                                                   | `toll`                               |
| Ford                | Entering the ford (0907), unless by boat                                                                                                                                                                              | oracle `ford`                        |
| Shrine              | Entering the shrine (1104), the first time in a trip (`trip.visits: 1`)                                                                                                                                               | `shrine`                             |
| **Landmark**        | Entering the Grey Stones (0503), or a discovered landmark: **no table, but `pause: true`: describe it and press Continue**                                                                                            | —                                    |
| Night encounter     | Camping in danger 2 or more, or entering a hex with danger in the dark (a night march), off the road                                                                                                                  | `encounter` (by night)               |
| Lost in the dark    | Each hex entered in the dark (a night march), off the road: 1–3 gets you lost                                                                                                                                         | `getting-lost` (+ Navigation)        |
| Foraging            | Pressing **Forage for food** (once a day; 3 hours, halves the rest of the day's march) in forest, dense forest, plains, farmland, heath or marsh                                                                      | `forage` (+ Survival)                |
| Hunger              | A day ending with no food left (`at: day-end`, `party.resources.food` below 1), when the party can't camp either; morale decides how it goes, down to desertion                                                       | `hunger` (+ Morale) → `desertion`    |
| Fishing             | Pressing **Fish** (by day, next to water or on the coast, once a day; 2 hours): a d6 at or under Survival, two days of food; 1–3, one                                                                                 | `fishing` (roll-under on its own d6) |
| A long day          | A day marched past its 8 hours (`trip.marched` over `system.hoursPerDay`: a night march after a full day): fatigue +1 as it ends                                                                                      | —                                    |
| Homesick            | The 10th, 20th and 30th dawn of a trip (`trip.day`): morale −1                                                                                                                                                        | —                                    |
| Going in circles    | Entering a hex for the third time in a trip (`trip.visits: 3`): morale −1                                                                                                                                             | —                                    |
| Out of the Greywood | Leaving the Greywood (`from.region`, `unless: hex.region`): morale +1                                                                                                                                                 | —                                    |

**To see Continue**: from Ashford click the Grey Stones (0503) and **Travel**. The trip stops on arrival with _Landmark: waiting for you_ and a **Continue** button in the trip panel; the journal says the same.
