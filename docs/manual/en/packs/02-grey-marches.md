# The Grey Marches

> The old kingdom ends where the road gives out. Past Ashford, the Keld Road crosses the river on a toll bridge and climbs to Fort Keld, whose garrison hasn't been paid in a year. North, the Greywood keeps its own counsel — lights between the trees at night, and something large asleep under the roots. South, the Hollow Hills ring with bandits, and the Saltmere lies flat and grey, crossed only by Brenna's ferry. East of the hills, nobody has drawn the map.

**The Grey Marches** is a small frontier to play and to learn from. It's a bundled pack (MIT) with its own **travel system** and **two example maps in one campaign**, and between them they use every feature of OpenTabletop. Read its files in the Oracle app as worked examples: each one has comments saying what it shows, and every table, oracle, generator and deck says in its description what it is for and which features it shows ("Shows: d66 dice…"), so the Oracle's list doubles as a tour.

## Playing it

1. In Map, **Maps → Example maps → The Grey Marches**. It opens as one of your maps; open it again later to go on, or start it fresh.
2. **Play** (<kbd>P</kbd>): the map opens ready, **With rules**, playing **The Grey Marches** (its system in **Map settings → Campaign → System**), with discovery on. Choose a season if you like (**New trip**) and click a destination: the party starts in Ashford. It's the Company: Kael, Mara and Old Tobin, in the **Characters** section of the trip's **Party** tab, where you choose who acts (whoever leads a ford, whoever tends the wounded) and see their health, the rations and gear they carry and their dread; the party's Survival, Charisma, Navigation and Stealth are theirs.
3. Try the road to Fort Keld (the toll), the path to the shrine (the ford), the trail to the Grey Stones (a landmark that waits for you), a night in the Greywood, or the ferry across the Saltmere: at Brenna's shore, switch the travel mode to **By boat**; it only goes on water and coast, so switch back to **On foot** to land.
4. Head east, into the blank hexes: they are discovered as you go (untick **Discover the map as you travel** to keep them blank).
5. **Maps → Example maps → The Hollow Hills**: the hills around Hollow Gate up close, drawn at 3 km a hex, in **the same campaign** (_The Grey Marches_, **Map settings → Campaign**): one world clock, the same factions, the Iron Clans holding land on both maps. The Company waits on the way out of Fort Keld: follow the trail past the clans' watch-cairn (a landmark) and over Stonewater Ford to the Barrow-halls, or up to the Moot-stone and the Raven Shrine. Time spent on one map has gone by on the other; one trip plays at a time, so starting one there asks to pause the other.
6. In the [Campaign app](../campaign/01-getting-started.md), _The Grey Marches_ lists both maps under **Maps**; its **Factions** tab shows the land on either (choose the map above the minimap).

The same system plays without a map in the Travel app.

## What is rolled, and when

Every check is in `travel.yaml`; the journal says each one as it comes up.

| Check                      | When                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Table                                |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Weather                    | Every dawn                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `sky` (weather model, by season)     |
| A stormy dawn              | Right after the dawn's weather check, if it rolled a storm: fatigue +1. An action, not a check: it rolls nothing, so the system takes it `on: checks.WEATHER_CHECK_REQUIRED` and reads the weather just rolled (`trip.weather: storm`)                                                                                              | —                                    |
| Omens                      | Every dawn on the road, not once at the destination (`unless: { trip.arrived: true }`): a card drawn, gone until the deck runs out, in the campaign's Oracle (draws by hand share it)                                                                                                                                               | `omens` (deck)                       |
| Getting lost               | Every dawn, unless you set off along a road or river, or by boat, or a storm keeps you in (decided after the weather at the same dawn: the checks of one moment are decided one by one)                                                                                                                                             | `getting-lost` (+ Navigation)        |
| Encounter                  | Entering by day a hex whose danger is over the party's Stealth (the Greywood, the Hollow Hills), unless you came by road; or resting where danger is 3 or more (`at: [trip.hex-enter, actions.rest]`, told apart by `trip.moment`)                                                                                                  | `encounter` (by day)                 |
| Toll                       | Entering Keld Bridge (0909) by road                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `toll`                               |
| Ford                       | Entering the ford (0907), unless by boat                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | oracle `ford`                        |

| Night encounter            | Camping in danger 2 or more, or entering a hex with danger in the dark (a night march), off the road                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | `encounter` (by night)               |
| Lost in the dark           | Each hex entered in the dark (a night march), off the road: 1–3 gets you lost                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | `getting-lost` (+ Navigation)        |
| Lights in the middle watch | Asleep in camp where danger is 3 or more with nobody on watch, as the middle watch begins (`at: time.watch.middle`, 23:00): every companion's Dread +1                                                                                                                                                                              | —                                    |
| Foraging                   | Pressing **Forage for food** (once a day; 3 hours, halves the rest of the day's march) in forest, dense forest, plains, farmland, heath or marsh, rolled once the three hours are done (`at: actions.forage.done`)                                                                                                                  | `forage` (+ Survival)                |
| Hunger                     | A day ending with no food left (`at: time.day-end`, `party.items.ration.count` below 1: the Company's and its companions' together), when the party can't camp either; morale decides how it goes, down to desertion                                                                                                                | `hunger` (+ Morale) → `desertion`    |
| Fishing                    | Pressing **Fish** (by day, next to water or on the coast, once a day): after its 2 hours a step rolls it (`roll: checks.FISHING_CHECK_REQUIRED`; the check has no **When**): a d6 at or under Survival, two days of food; 1–3, one; when nothing bites, the step after it says so (it waits for the roll and reads `today.skunked`) | `fishing` (roll-under on its own d6) |
| A long day                 | A day marched past its 8 hours (`trip.marched` over `system.hoursPerDay`: a night march after a full day): fatigue +1 as it ends                                                                                                                                                                                                    | —                                    |
| Homesick                   | The 10th, 20th and 30th dawn of a trip (`trip.day`): morale −1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | —                                    |
| Going in circles           | Entering a hex for the third time in a trip (`trip.visits: 3`): morale −1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | —                                    |
| Out of the Greywood        | Leaving the Greywood: morale +1. An action the system takes `on: trip.hex-enter`, reading the hex left as the moment's checks do (`from.region`, `unless: hex.region`)                                                                                                                                                              | —                                    |

**To see Continue**: from Ashford click the Grey Stones (0503) and **March**. The trip stops on arrival with _Landmark at The Grey Stones: waiting for you_ (a check that waits says the hex it came up in) and a **Continue** button in the trip panel; **What just happened** and the journal say the same.
