# Core

**Core** is the generic pack every app ships with: tools for any game, with no setting, and the **Generic rules**, the smallest travel system, the one a map plays when it chooses none. It's MIT-licensed, written in English with a Spanish translation.

| Definition                                      | What it's for                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Yes or no?** (oracle)                         | Ask a yes/no question and choose how likely it is. The classic solo-play oracle.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **How do they take it?** (oracle)               | How someone answers a request: the bigger the ask, the harder the yes. Every answer comes with a **Motive**.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Prompt** (generator)                          | An action and a subject (`{{action.text}} {{subject.text}}`) to spark an idea when you're stuck.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Scene twist** (table)                         | At the start of a scene: on a 1 it doesn't go as expected. Offers advantage when things are calm, disadvantage when tension is high.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Advantage** and **Disadvantage** (roll modes) | Roll twice and keep the higher or the lower total. Core's tables offer them, and so can any pack that depends on Core (`modes: [roll-modes.advantage, roll-modes.disadvantage]`).                                                                                                           |
| **Twists** and **Complications**                | What changes, and a deck of complications to draw from (a card stays out until you reshuffle).                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **A faction's turn** (table)                    | What a faction does on a world turn, for any game: expands (+1 hex), holds, schemes or loses ground. A system's factions roll it with `turn: core/table.faction-turn`.                                                                                                                      |
| **A growing threat** (clocks)                   | A progress clock for any game: the threat grows a segment at a time and, when full, starts over. A system brings it with `clocks: core/clocks.threat`.                                                                                                                                      |
| **The threat strikes** (world rules)            | The world's reaction to it, the smallest one: when the threat fills (`on: world.clocks.the-threat-grows.full`), a line on the timeline and one of the **Twists**, then the world waits for you to think about it (`pause:`: **Go on**). A system brings it with `world: core/world.threat`. |

Every one is **tagged** with what it's about (`scenes`, `inspiration`, `questions`, `people`; _A faction's turn_ also `factions` and `rules`, since a system's world turn rolls it), so the Oracle's **Filter** finds them: `−rules` leaves what you'd roll by hand.

## The Generic rules

Core's system (`system.yaml`, `id: default`, so its id is `core`) is the smallest one that plays: a map whose campaign chooses no system plays it (**Map settings → Campaign → System**), and so do maps and trips of older versions that played the rules built into the apps. Like any bundled system, the Systems app shows it read-only; **New system** starts from a copy of its rules, to change as you like. For one that uses everything, see [The Grey Marches](02-grey-marches.md).

| Part                                                   | What it does, and which feature it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Generic rules** (system)                             | Names its parts: `travel: travel-rules.default`, `bindings: bindings.default`, `calendar: calendar.default`, `weather: [weather.default]`, `land: [land.default]`, `sheet: sheet.character`, `partySheet: sheet.party`, `items: [items.gear]`, `bestiary: [bestiary.foes]`. A system that names no `travel` doesn't travel; this one does.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Travel rules** (`travel.yaml`)                       | Played at 10 km a hex (`travel: { hexKm: 10 }`), on foot (30 km a day) or on horseback (50 km), across the terrains of the Hexmapper's palettes, faster on roads and trails; water isn't crossed, and a lake only on the ice in winter (`passable: { when: { time.season: winter } }`). Its day is named by its calendar's parts: trips start as the morning begins (`start: time.part.morning`), and a party that can't march camps from the night (`halt: { at: time.part.night, do: actions.camp }`). Food is a value of the party's sheet (below), eaten a day at a time as each day ends. One check, **Weather** (`WEATHER_CHECK_REQUIRED`), as each morning begins (`at: time.part.morning`), which the bindings roll on _The plain sky_ (`weather: weather.default`); rain makes the march ×0.75, a storm or snow ×0.5 (`weather: { rain: { speed: 0.75 } … }`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **March**, **Camp**, **Rest**, **Eat**, **Explore**    | Five actions made of steps. **March** walks while it isn't dark for the day's 8 hours (`when: { time.tags: { not: dark }, trip.marched: { lt: '{{system.hoursPerDay}}' } }`), to the destination or one hex (a parameter, `travel.move: '{{params.until}}'`), and the trip takes it when it goes on by itself (`travel.onward`). **Camp** sleeps until the morning (`time: time.part.morning`); **Rest** is an hour, only by day; **Eat** is taken by the system itself as each day ends (`on: time.day-end`), using 1 food of the party's sheet (`party.values.food: -1`), and when there was none left the journal says “There is nothing left to eat.” (a `say` step with its own condition, `when: { trip.below: party.values.food }`: a later step sees what the one before it changed). **Explore** is an hour by day, then an idea of what the party finds rolled on Core's **Prompt** (`roll: generator.prompt`: a step that rolls something of the Oracle, its result in the journal by its name), and then _A place found?_ (`roll: table.place-found`), which on a 6 makes a **Landmark** on the party's hex. The Generic rules have no progress clocks, so no moments of the world (`world.clocks.<id>.full`): the Grey Marches show them. The steps that wait for the player (`pause`, `ask`), change the way of travelling or wait for something are left to systems that need them (the Grey Marches' ferry): see [Actions and steps](../technical/10-actions-and-steps.md). |
| **Despair**                                            | The smallest moment written as a condition (a reaction): as the party's morale reaches 0 (`on: { when: { party.values.morale: { lte: 0 } } }`), whatever lowered it, the trip stops (`pause:`) for you to decide; once, until morale rises and falls to 0 again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **The plain sky** (`weather.yaml`)                     | The smallest weather model for any setting: clear, cloudy, rain, storm and, in winter only, snow, today's following yesterday's by chance tables per season (`next`), so rain lasts a few days and storms blow over. A kind a season leaves out (snow in spring) starts over from the season's `start`. Shows [weather with inertia](../systems/13-weather.md); the Grey Marches show a hex flower too.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **The plain land** (`land.yaml`)                       | The smallest land model: plains, farmland and forests, hills rising into mountains, lakes and their marshes, each tending to go on as itself (`next`), mostly open land where nothing is known (`start`). The Hexmapper's **Generate** fills empty hexes with it; discovery stays off. Shows [land models](../technical/07-kinds.md#land-models).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **The plain calendar** (`calendar.yaml`)               | Four seasons of 90 days, one month each; a day in four parts (morning from 06:00, afternoon, evening, night from 20:00, tagged `dark`: `time.part`, `time.tags`) and six watches of four hours (`time.watch`, 1 to 6; the World panel's **Next: …**).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Character** and **Party** (sheets)                   | The smallest sheets: a character's **Health** (3, never below 0 nor above 3, drawn as a bar) and **Wounded**, for you to track by hand, with the two parts that hold by themselves at their smallest: **Harm**, made of Health (`from: '{{3 - self.values.health}}'`), and **Down**, while Health is 0 (`when: { self.values.health: { lte: 0 } }`); the party's own **Morale** (3, from 0 to 5), drawn as boxes (`display: boxes`), and its **Food** (6 for a new trip, never below 0): what the party carries, as a plain value of its sheet, the smallest way. The smallest characters for the roster too: **New character** in the [Characters app](../characters/01-getting-started.md) with the Generic rules makes one, and a trip with them takes it with **From the roster**; a **Party** made there (**Sheet**: _a party_) is the smallest party to carry from trip to trip, its Food left as it was.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Gear** (`items.yaml`)                                | The smallest item catalogue: a rope, a torch and a ration, with tags (`climbing`, `light`, `food`), on a sheet that gives items no fields nor places, so they're a plain list with counts, ten things in all (the sheet's `items.room: 10` with `over: refuse`: what's given past it is left behind, said in the journal). **Add an item** on a character's card offers them; effects can give them (`acting.items.torch: 1`). Shows [items](../systems/08-items.md); the Grey Marches show fields, places and containers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Foes** and **Trouble on the road** (`bestiary.yaml`) | The smallest [bestiary](../technical/07-kinds.md#bestiaries): one template, **Foe**, on the characters' own sheet, its Health rolled for each copy (`'{{1d3}}'`); and a table that makes them, as many as its `set` rolls and its text says (`present.foe: '{{foes}}'`). Rolled by hand during a trip, the foes are among those present (`present.foe.count`); one whose Health reaches 0 is **Down**, and gone (the sheet's _Down_ `leaves`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Places** and **A place found?** (`places.yaml`)      | The smallest [catalogue of places](../technical/07-kinds.md#places): one template, **Landmark**, with a text, an icon of the map's (`game:obelisk`) and the tag it gives its hex (`landmark`); and a table that makes it (`effects: { hex.pois.landmark: 1 }`), rolled by **Explore**. On a map in the Hexmapper it becomes a point of interest **Made from** _Landmark_; in a trip without a map, a line in the journal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

**In a campaign.** A [campaign](../campaign/01-getting-started.md) that plays the Generic rules (a new one in the Campaign app, or any map whose campaign chooses no system) gets a world clock in the plain calendar: its date in seasons, **Next: …** by its parts of the day and its watches, its weather rolled day by day on _The plain sky_ (a trip's, while one goes on), events and progress clocks you add by hand. The Generic rules declare no factions and no clocks of their own, since powers and threats belong to a setting: a system of yours brings Core's **A growing threat** (`clocks: core/clocks.threat`, and what it does when full with `world: core/world.threat`) and gives its factions **A faction's turn** (`turn: core/table.faction-turn`). For a campaign with all of it (two maps, factions, clocks, events), see [The Grey Marches](02-grey-marches.md).

Other packs depend on Core (the Grey Marches use its tables), so it isn't copied in place: **Duplicate as a new pack** in the Oracle app makes a pack of yours from it (`core-mine`), to change as you like, while the bundled Core stays for the packs that need it and keeps its updates. A copy of Core made by an older version still replaces it; the Systems app says when a system uses one older than the bundled Core and lets you take its changes.
