# The Iron Clans

Hill-folk of the east, who hold the land beyond the Keld ([[faction:grey-marches/iron-clans]]). They trade iron for salt, and take what isn’t traded.

They want Fort Keld empty. They fear what’s behind [[Hollow Gate#Secrets|Hollow Gate]].
