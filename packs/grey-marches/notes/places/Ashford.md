# Ashford

A market town where the road begins ([[map:greymarches1#0608]]). Mud in the street, wool in the stalls, and news at the inn for the price of a drink.

- **The Wet Ferret**, the inn: ask for [[The Wet Ferret|news]] (a page not written yet: a click makes it).
- **Old Brenna’s smithy**: not to be confused with [[Brenna the ferrywoman]], her niece.
- The Vale’s men collect the toll here on market days.

## Rumours heard

- Lights in the Greywood after the second watch.
- The ferry hasn’t crossed since the last moon.
