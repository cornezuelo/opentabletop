# The ferry

A flat boat across the Saltmere ([[map:greymarches1#1011]]), the only way over the salt lake. [[Brenna the ferrywoman]] works it, when she’s paid and the lake is calm.

Crossing costs a coin a head; a horse is two. When the salt-wind blows, nobody crosses.

**Shows:** a place linked from its cell on the map (its note is `places/The ferry`).
