# Hollow Gate

A door in the hillside ([[map:greymarches1#1815]]), iron gone black, warm to the touch in winter.

## What’s known

The [[The Iron Clans|Iron Clans]] won’t camp within sight of it. The garrison of Fort Keld ([[map:greymarches1#1610]]) used to send a patrol past it every moon.

## Secrets

The door opens from inside, once a year, on the night the Wyrm’s clock fills. Whatever comes out remembers the party’s names.
