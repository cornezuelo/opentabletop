# Welcome to Notes

This page is yours: change it, keep it as a cheat sheet or delete it. It's in **General**, where what belongs to no campaign goes; each campaign has its own pages, under its name on the left.

## What goes here

The lore of your games: people, places, factions, what the party knows. The other apps keep only what the rules play with; a character, a cell, a place, a faction or a campaign has a **note**, the path of its page here, and **↗** beside it opens that page.

## Writing

**Edit** (the pencil, or `Ctrl+E`) writes a page, **Read** (the eye) shows it. It's kept as you type.

## Links

Type `[[` and choose: pages, a heading of a page (after `#`), and the things of your games.

- `[[Old Tobin]]` — a page by its title; `[[npcs/Old Tobin]]` by its path; `[[Old Tobin|the ferryman]]` with another text; `[[Old Tobin#Debts]]` a heading of it.
- `[[character:kael]]`, `[[map:<id>#0608]]`, `[[faction:<campaign>/<id>]]`, `[[campaign:<id>]]` — a character, a cell of a map, a faction, a campaign: each opens in its app.
- A link to a page not written yet is dashed: a click makes it.

## Folders and campaigns

A page's path is its folders and its title: `npcs/Old Tobin`. Change it at the top of the page to rename or move it, and the links that reached it follow. **Campaign** moves it to another campaign, or to General.

## Beside each page

What links to it (pages, and the notes of its campaign's Log), what of the games it names or points at it, and where its title is written without a link (**Link** makes those links).

## And

- **⋯ → Export as .md files** downloads every page, for Obsidian or SilverBullet; **Import .md files…** brings them back.
- **⋯ → Add the example pages of…** adds the pages a pack brings, such as the Grey Marches'.
- **?**, at the top right, opens the whole manual.
